Projects

Metric

I built Metric with my brother. It helps recruiting teams compare builders using evidence from their work. It has covered more than 30,000 people and predicted two of the top three hackathon winners in one backtest.

Catan Zero

Catan Zero is a reinforcement-learning project for training models to play Catan. I built it with Michael Tan, who is number one in Catan Elo. We met through YC fellows after he showed me a model trained on scraped Catan games.

Other projects

  • SF Graffiti Search: semantic search over 87,790 San Francisco inspector photos. Each image was described by Gemini so the collection could be searched by content, style, or location.
  • HumanPaste: retypes clipboard text with realistic keystrokes for terminals, remote sessions, and apps that accept typing but not direct paste.
  • Awake: keeps a Mac awake during long AI agent runs.
  • Ultrathink: a drug-discovery search system using several structure and molecule models together.
  • DateGuesser: a weekday calculation trainer built around Conway's Doomsday algorithm.
  • BoidDynamics: an interactive flocking and complex-systems simulation.
