Building it

Running hundreds of assessments at once across hundreds of VMs was a system design problem. At roughly 200 million tokens a minute, we had to keep runs from getting lost or corrupted.

Bot detection, outreach, and our CRM were hard too.

How it started

I started by testing a site so I could study for exams for free. I found exposed information on roughly 500,000 people and received $2,500 for reporting it.

Later Peak findings paid up to roughly $30,000 and led to FBI investigations into other hackers.
