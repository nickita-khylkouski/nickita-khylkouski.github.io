The Doomsday rule on one page

The method

  1. Century anchor. Look up the first two digits below. 1900s: Wednesday. 2000s: Tuesday.
  2. Year’s doomsday. Add the anchor to the year formula. Take the result mod 7.
  3. A doomsday in the month. Use the memorized date, or one 7, 14, 21 or 28 days away.
  4. Count. Add (or subtract) the days from that doomsday to your date, mod 7.

Weekday numbers

One doomsday per month

From April on, the even months repeat the number: 4/4, 6/6, 8/8, 10/10, 12/12. The odd ones pair up as “I work 9 to 5 at the 7-Eleven”: 5/9, 9/5, 7/11, 11/7. July 4, Halloween, Boxing Day and (outside leap years) Valentine’s Day are doomsdays too.

The year formula

Let y be the last two digits of the year.

ay ÷ 12, whole part
by ÷ 12, remainder
cb ÷ 4, whole part

doomsday = anchor + a + b + c (mod 7)

It gives the same answer as anchor + y + ⌊y/4⌋. Dividing by 12 first keeps the numbers small.
